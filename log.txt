However, restructure the application around **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**. The system has two distinct portals: **Doctor Portal** and **Receptionist Portal**. Each role must have its own navigation, dashboard, and permitted features.

### 1. Doctor Portal
All doctor-related and administrative medical operations must be accessible exclusively through the Doctor Portal.

- Dashboard
- Patient Management
- Medical Records
- Appointments
- Prescriptions
- Billing and Payments
- Profile
The Doctor Portal should contain the existing in-depth medical management functionality. Do not expose these administrative and clinical management features to receptionists.

### 2. Receptionist Portal
The Receptionist Portal should have a simplified, operational interface focused on front-desk activities.

**Overview Dashboard**

Include the following elements:

- **Start Shift:** Allow the receptionist to start their working shift.
- **Estimated End Time:** Display the expected shift completion time based on the shift start time and configured working hours.
- **Enroll Patient:** A prominent action to register a new patient.
- **Current Patient Queue:** Display the current number of patients waiting for consultation.
- **Create Appointment:** A quick-action button for creating appointments.
**Receptionist Navigation**

- Overview
- Enroll Patient
- Appointments
- Medical Records
- Billing
- Profile
Remove **Team Access** entirely from the Receptionist Portal.

### 3. Access Control Requirements

- Doctor and Receptionist must have separate portal experiences.
- Navigation items must be determined by the authenticated user's role.
- Receptionists must not be able to access doctor-only administrative or clinical management features.
- Doctors retain access to the full medical management functionality.
- Do not simply hide unauthorized navigation items; enforce role-based authorization at the route and backend API levels as well.

### 4. Design Requirements

- Preserve the current visual design and styling.
- Maintain a consistent design system across both portals.
- Keep the Receptionist Portal minimal, clean, and task-oriented.
- Avoid unnecessary UI elements, excessive descriptions, or decorative components.
- Ensure each dashboard prioritizes the actions relevant to its respective role.
**Important:** This is a restructuring of the existing application, not a request to redesign it from scratch. Reuse the existing components and styling wherever possible.
